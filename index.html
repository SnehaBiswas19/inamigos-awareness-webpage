01 / WHO WE ARE

Compassion in action.
Hope in every step.

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organisation founded in 2020. Its initiatives focus on community development and social empowerment, bringing people together to help address needs in education, basic support, animal welfare, women's empowerment, environmental care and skill development.
